# Change Log

## 0.1.0

First version.

- Opens `.pptx`, `.pptm`, `.ppsx`, `.ppsm`, `.potx` and `.potm` files in a read-only editor tab, drawn inside VS Code.
- Reloads when the file changes on disk and keeps the slide, zoom, scroll position and search.
- Thumbnails, speaker notes, marks for hidden slides.
- Find in all slides with match case, whole word and regular expressions.
- Zoom from 10% to 400%, fit slide, fit width; Ctrl/Cmd + wheel and pinch.
- Web, mail and slide links.
- English and Japanese interface.
